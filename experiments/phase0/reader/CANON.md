# Reading desk — simulation corpus

These fictional sources are assumed effective only in this exercise.

- **R-01.** An entry becomes complete only when the reader explicitly finishes it. Opening or previewing an entry leaves completion unchanged.
- **R-02.** Suggestions may appear only after the reader explicitly requests suggestions. This applies at every entry point.
- **R-03.** Reading and library browsing have distinct surfaces. Browsing belongs to the library. The boundary for a passive navigation link is not further specified.
- **R-04.** The active archive book, `ARCHIVE.md`, defines archive ordering. Load it when a change affects archive ordering.
- **R-05.** Focus should feel quiet. Passive informational density is a soft concern to monitor. It does not create a numerical limit on labels.

The human product owner establishes new identity norms. Existing norms may be interpreted by Council. Source acceptance for a real product requires a separately verified human decision.
